# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Setuper contributors and maintainers are committed to a respectful,
harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, appearance, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not people.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing levels of experience.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and repair harm where possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Personal insults, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions,
community spaces, and public interactions where someone represents Setuper.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to
[devwithvall@gmail.com](mailto:devwithvall@gmail.com). Do not include private,
sensitive, or security-related details in a public issue.

Maintainers will limit report details to people needed to investigate and
respond. They may request additional context and will consider the safety and
privacy of everyone involved.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation,
temporarily suspend access, or permanently exclude a participant. Responses
will be proportionate to the severity, impact, and pattern of behavior.

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately.
Maintainers who have a conflict of interest must recuse themselves from handling
the report.
